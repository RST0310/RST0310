Why AI in healthcare fails without workflow discipline

Author: Sai Teja Rayabarapu

Published in: The Hindu

Publication date: May 3, 2026

Article type: Professional / Trade Article

About the Article

This article examines why healthcare AI initiatives can struggle when technology is introduced without clear workflow alignment, operational requirements, validation controls, and human oversight. It focuses on the importance of deploying AI as part of a governed healthcare workflow rather than treating AI as a standalone tool.

AI Cricket Narrator: Bridging the Gap between Human and AI Commentary for Enhanced Viewer Experience

Authors: Sai Teja Rayabarapu and Manaswini Kodela

Institution: University of North Texas

Course: INFO 5731: Computational Methods for Information Systems

Work type: Graduate Coursework Technical Report

Completed: May 9, 2024

About this work

This graduate coursework research project explored artificial intelligence approaches for cricket commentary and examined the relationship between human and AI-generated commentary with the goal of improving viewer experience.
